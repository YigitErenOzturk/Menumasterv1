About MenuMaster

MenuMaster is a discovery platform that helps you find restaurants faster and smarter. Filter by city, explore menus, and pick the best spot for your next meal — all in one place.

Our Mission

Make restaurant discovery simple: clear menus, real ratings, and quick decisions.

Why MenuMaster?

Our Promise

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about MenuMaster

How does MenuMaster get restaurant data? ⌄

We fetch restaurant details from our API and display them in a clean, searchable format. This includes menu links, ratings, and location information when available.

Can I filter restaurants by city? ⌄

Yes. Use the city filter to quickly narrow down restaurants based on your selected city.

Do I need an account to use MenuMaster? ⌄

No account is required for browsing. You can explore restaurants and view menus instantly.

What if a restaurant has missing info (image/city/rating)? ⌄

Some restaurants may not provide complete details. In those cases, MenuMaster shows safe defaults (like a placeholder image) and keeps the listing usable.

How can I report an issue or suggest a restaurant? ⌄

You can contact us via the “Help” or “Contact” section on the site. We review feedback regularly.
